# Beacon — 2-Minute Hackathon Video Script & Shot List

**Target runtime:** 110-115 seconds.  
**Submission rule:** video must be 2:00 or less.  
**Goal:** show that Beacon is not a directory. It is an agentic talent-acceleration engine that routes employees to experts, mentors, sponsors, bootcamps, communities, and measurable outcomes.

## Core story

Follow one person: **Maria Oliveira**, a Cloud Solution Architect in Recife, Brazil. She wants to become a Principal Cloud Solution Architect in AI. The emotional problem is not that Maria lacks ambition or talent; it is that the right people and opportunities are distributed across geography, org boundaries, languages, and hidden networks.

## Voiceover script

### 0:00-0:10 — Problem

**Visual:** Open on the Accelerate view (the app's default — there is no separate landing page). Rest on Maria's goal card: Maria Oliveira · Recife, Brazil · 3-Year Goal → Become a Principal Cloud Solution Architect in AI.

**Narration:**  
"Maria knows where she wants to go: Principal Cloud Solution Architect in AI. But in a global company, the people who can help her are spread across orgs, regions, languages, and communities she may never discover."

**Caption:** Talent is everywhere. Opportunity is not.

### 0:10-0:22 — Reframe

**Visual:** Stay on the goal card; let the subtitle — "connects you to the people and experiences that move it forward" — read on screen.

**Narration:**  
"Beacon reimagines expert networking as an agentic talent-acceleration engine. Instead of another directory, it turns a career goal or urgent technical need into the right experts, mentors, sponsors, learning pathways, and communities."

**Caption:** From recommendation to routed connection.

### 0:22-0:52 — Accelerate: the plan

**Visual:** Click **Build my plan**. Briefly show the skill-signal bars, then move down the six opportunity cards under "6 ways to accelerate." Hover one **Why this match?** reason.

**Narration:**  
"Here, Beacon analyzes Maria's goal and finds six concrete next steps: a mentor who made the same move, a peer solving related AI architecture problems, a sponsor who can open stretch work, a shadowing opportunity, a cohort, and the HOLA community. Every recommendation includes the reason, and every person or opportunity is opt-in."

**Caption:** Mentors. Peers. Sponsors. Shadowing. Cohorts. Communities.

### 0:52-1:10 — Agent drafts, human approves

**Visual:** Click **Draft all 6 introductions**. Show the "6 introductions drafted for you" review panel with the per-person messages, then click **Approve & send all 6**.

**Narration:**  
"One action drafts a personalized introduction for every match. But Beacon doesn't send blindly — Maria reviews each message and approves before anything goes out, and each recipient can still accept or decline."

**Caption:** Agentic orchestration. Human approval.

### 1:10-1:24 — The agentic loop

**Visual:** On the confirmed state, show "Your acceleration network is in motion," then the **How is the mentorship going?** follow-up. Click one option to reveal the **Next best action** card.

**Narration:**  
"After the connection, Beacon follows up: did it help, did development start, is more support needed? It updates the plan and recommends the next best action — a continuous loop, not a one-time match."

**Caption:** Understand. Act. Observe. Adapt.

### 1:24-1:40 — Urgent expert routing

**Visual:** Switch to **Connect**. The ask reads: "I need someone who understands Dataverse DLP, speaks Portuguese, and can help today." Click **Find connections**; show the understood signals (Portuguese · Today · Live discussion) and the ranked people, each with **Why this match?**

**Narration:**  
"The same engine handles urgent expert routing: describe the outcome, and Beacon returns people ordered by relevance, language, availability, and declared expertise. It explains why each match was found and drafts warm outreach."

**Caption:** Smart expert bench by domain, language, and availability.

### 1:40-1:54 — Measurable impact

**Visual:** Switch to **Impact**. Pan the metric tiles — reach, connections, needs resolved, languages, and **unanswered requests** — then the momentum and capacity charts and the "Beacon complements your talent investments" strip. Optionally flash **How this is measured**.

**Narration:**  
"Leaders get the missing feedback loop: participation, reach, skilling outcomes, unanswered requests, and expert-capacity gaps — starting with the HOLA pilot. Every number is transparent about how it's measured, and Beacon complements the talent systems you already run."

**Caption:** Real-time visibility into opportunity flow.

### 1:54-2:00 — Close

**Visual:** End on the confirmed acceleration network with the closing tagline on screen.

**Narration:**  
"Beacon helps every employee find the right human at the right moment."

**Caption:** The right human. The right opportunity. The right moment.

## Recording plan

1. Record the deployed Demo build (default and only mode on the hosted app). Primary path: **Accelerate (build → draft → approve → follow-up) → Connect → Impact**.
2. Record in a **wide window** so the left sidebar (Beacon brand + Trust & transparency) is visible; a narrow window collapses to a bottom nav and hides the brand.
3. Do not show Live API or Production modes — the mode toggle is hidden in the hosted build.
4. Keep every claim tied to visible UI: six opportunities with reasons, drafted introductions plus the approve step, the follow-up next-best-action, ranked Connect matches, the Impact dashboard with the Career Hub strip, and the Trust modal.
5. Record at 1080p, 16:9, dark theme, browser zoom around 90-100%, notifications off.
